# FlowGT Recorder Privacy Policy · 流录隐私政策

Draft of 2026-11-21, for the Microsoft Store listing. To be published at a public URL that Gabriel chooses.
Technical background for developers: `docs/PRIVACY.md`.

## English

**Effective date:** (date of publication)

FlowGT Recorder ("the app") records your screen, camera and microphone into video files on your computer.
This policy explains what the app does with your data. In short: **we collect nothing.**

**1. We do not collect data.** The app has no account, no server of ours, no analytics, no telemetry, no crash
reporting, no advertising and no update check of its own (updates come through the Microsoft Store). We, the
developers, never receive your recordings, screenshots, settings or any information about how you use the app.

**2. Your recordings stay on your computer.** Recordings and screenshots are saved in a folder on your PC
(by default `Videos\FlowGT Recorder`, changeable in Settings). Unfinished recordings are kept in a hidden work
folder next to it until they are saved, so they can be recovered after a crash. Settings are stored in the
app's own data folder. You can delete any of these files at any time; uninstalling the app removes its settings.

**3. Camera and microphone.** The camera is used only to show your picture in a corner of the recording and in
the preview; the microphone only to record your voice into the recording. They are used only while you are
recording or looking at the preview, and nothing is sent anywhere. Windows asks for your permission the first
time, and you can turn either off in Windows Settings → Privacy & security.

**4. Screen.** The screen (or the area you select) is captured only while you are recording or taking a
screenshot.

**5. Google Drive (optional, only if you sign in).** If you choose to sign in with your Google account, the app
can upload finished recordings to a "FlowGT Recorder" folder in **your own** Google Drive. The app asks only
for the `drive.file` permission, so it can see only the files it created, not the rest of your Drive. The
upload goes directly from your computer to Google; it does not pass through any server of ours. Google's own
privacy policy applies to data stored in your Drive. You can sign out at any time in Settings; signing out
stops all uploads. If you never sign in, nothing leaves your computer.

**6. Children.** The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

**7. Changes.** If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.

**8. Contact.** (contact address to be added before publication)

## 中文

**生效日期：**（发布当天）

流录（下称“本应用”）把你的屏幕、摄像头和麦克风录成你电脑上的视频文件。本政策说明本应用如何对待你的数据。
一句话：**我们不收集任何数据。**

**1. 我们不收集数据。** 本应用没有账号、没有我们的服务器、没有统计分析、没有遥测、没有崩溃上报、没有广告，
也不自己检查更新（更新通过微软商店）。开发者永远不会收到你的录像、截图、设置，或任何关于你如何使用本应用的信息。

**2. 录像只存在你的电脑上。** 录像和截图存在你电脑的一个文件夹里（默认“视频\FlowGT Recorder”，可在设置里改）。
还没存好的录像放在旁边一个隐藏的工作文件夹里，存好后删除，这样电脑死机后还能找回。设置存在本应用自己的数据文件夹。
这些文件你随时可以删除；卸载本应用会删掉它的设置。

**3. 摄像头和麦克风。** 摄像头只用来在录像的一角和预览里显示你的画面；麦克风只用来把你的声音录进录像。只在你录制
或看预览时使用，不发送到任何地方。第一次使用时 Windows 会征求你的同意，你也可以在“Windows 设置 → 隐私和安全性”
里随时关掉。

**4. 屏幕。** 只在你录制或截图时抓取屏幕（或你框选的区域）。

**5. Google Drive（可选，只有登录后才有）。** 如果你选择用自己的 Google 账号登录，本应用可以把录好的录像上传到
**你自己** Google Drive 里的“FlowGT Recorder”文件夹。本应用只申请 `drive.file` 权限，只能看到它自己建的文件，
看不到你 Drive 里的其他内容。上传直接从你的电脑发到 Google，不经过我们的任何服务器。存在你 Drive 里的数据适用
Google 自己的隐私政策。你可以随时在设置里退出登录，退出后不再上传。不登录，就什么都不会离开你的电脑。

**6. 儿童。** 本应用不收集任何人的信息，包括儿童。

**7. 变更。** 本政策如有变化，新版本会发布在本网址，并注明新的日期。

**8. 联系方式。**（发布前补上联系地址）
